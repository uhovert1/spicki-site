9. Как мы защищаем данные

  • Сайт и сервис брони работают только по защищённому соединению HTTPS.
  • Доступ к брони есть только у сотрудников Spički — по личному логину и паролю. Пароли хранятся в зашифрованном виде, вход действует одну смену (12 часов).
  • Официанты видят брони без номеров телефонов.
  • Код в ссылке на бронь хранится только в виде хеша, поэтому даже из базы данных ссылку восстановить нельзя.
  • Все изменения брони записываются в журнал: кто и когда создал, подтвердил, перенёс или отменил бронь.
  • При утечке данных, которая создаёт риск для вас, мы уведомим Повереника в установленный Законом срок, а при высоком риске — и вас.

9. How we protect data

  • The website and booking service work only over secure HTTPS connections.
  • Only Spički staff can access bookings, each with a personal login and password. Passwords are stored encrypted; a sign-in lasts one shift (12 hours).
  • Waiters see bookings without phone numbers.
  • The code in your booking link is stored only as a hash, so the link cannot be rebuilt even from the database.
  • Every change to a booking is logged: who created, confirmed, moved or cancelled it, and when.
  • If a data breach puts you at risk, we will notify the Commissioner within the deadline set by the Law, and you as well where the risk is high.

10. Your rights

At any time you can:

  • find out what data we hold about you and get a copy;
  • correct inaccurate data;
  • ask us to delete your data — for example right after your visit, without waiting 12 months;
  • restrict or object to processing;
  • receive your data in a machine-readable format.

Write to info@spickibg.com or message us on Instagram @spicki_bg. We reply within 30 days. You can also cancel a booking yourself using the link you received after booking.

If you believe we are infringing your rights, you can lodge a complaint with the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection (Poverenik), Bulevar kralja Aleksandra 15, Belgrade, poverenik.rs.

11. Age

The website and venue are for guests aged 18 and over. If we learn that a booking was made by a minor, we will delete their data.

12. Data about other people

If you book a table for someone else and give their name or phone number, please let them know and point them to this policy.

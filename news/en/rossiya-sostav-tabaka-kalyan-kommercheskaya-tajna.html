Russia ends trade secrecy on tobacco ingredients, hookah tobacco included. What it means for Belgrade

18 November 2026

Russia's State Duma has passed a law under which data on harmful ingredients in tobacco and nicotine products can no longer be kept as a trade secret. Here is what it means for hookah tobacco and for hookah lounge guests in Belgrade.

What happened

On 11 November 2026 Russia's State Duma unanimously passed bill No. 1286415-8 in its second and third readings. It amends the law on trade secrets: data on the presence and quantity of certain ingredients in tobacco and nicotine products is added to the list of information companies may not keep secret. Interfax, RIA Novosti, Vedomosti, Izvestia and Parlamentskaya Gazeta all reported the vote.

The rule covers substances that increase nicotine dependence, are toxic, carcinogenic, mutagenic or harmful to reproduction, or acquire such properties when heated or smouldering. Duma speaker Vyacheslav Volodin said ingredient information should be open rather than hidden behind trade secrecy.

When it takes effect

At the second reading the start date was moved from 1 March to 1 January 2027, according to Parlamentskaya Gazeta. The bill was introduced by the government. It still needs approval from the Federation Council and the president's signature; no such step had been reported at the time of writing.

According to PPT.ru, manufacturers and importers already report product composition to the authorities every year under the Eurasian Economic Union technical regulation. The new law does not create a new report; it removes the right to label part of that data a trade secret. Reports on the vote do not describe how the information will reach buyers.

Where hookah comes in

Coverage of the law focuses on vapes and cigarettes, and hookah is not mentioned separately. But the wording applies to all tobacco products, and Russia's technical regulation law on tobacco products (No. 268-FZ) expressly defines hookah tobacco as a type of smoking tobacco product. In our reading, therefore, the rule also covers shisha tobacco sold in Russia. How it works in practice will become clear once the law is in force.

What it means for Serbia and Belgrade

Nothing changes for Serbia: the law applies only in Russia, and tobacco sold in Serbia is governed by Serbian rules. For comparison, in the European Union manufacturers and importers already report ingredients and their quantities to the authorities through the shared EU-CEG system, and the non-confidential part of that data is published.

For hookah lounge guests the point is the trend. Regulators in more and more countries want buyers to be able to find out what exactly they are smoking. If you like to know your tobaccos, it pays to read the packaging and the manufacturer's information. Hookah is for adults only, and that rule applies everywhere.

FAQ

Will hookah tobacco ingredients have to be disclosed in Russia?

The law passed by the Duma bars keeping data on harmful ingredients secret for all tobacco products, and Russian law classes hookah tobacco as a smoking tobacco product. The Federation Council and the president still need to approve it.

When does Russia's tobacco and vape ingredients law take effect?

On 1 January 2027, provided the Federation Council approves it and the president signs it.

Does anything change for hookah lounges in Belgrade?

No. The Russian law applies only in Russia; hookah lounges in Serbia operate under Serbian rules.

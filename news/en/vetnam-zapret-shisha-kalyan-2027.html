Vietnam bans shisha (hookah) from 1 July 2027: what it means for Belgrade and Serbia

22 November 2026

Vietnam's National Assembly has passed amendments to its tobacco law: from 1 July 2027 the country will ban not only the trade in but also the use of shisha (hookah), as well as e-cigarettes, heated tobacco and nicotine pouches.

What happened

On 21 November 2026 Vietnam's National Assembly passed an amended Law on Prevention and Control of Tobacco Harm. It was approved by 465 of 481 deputies. The law changes 12 of the 35 articles of the existing act, and most of it takes effect on 1 July 2027, with some provisions applying later.

For the hookah world, the key part is the new list of prohibitions. Shisha, e-cigarettes, heated tobacco and nicotine pouches are banned from production, trade, import and export, storage, transport and use. According to the Vietnamese outlets Dân Trí and DTiNews, the ban also covers raw materials for these products and the devices and parts used with them. The law uses the loanword "shisha"; the reports give no definition, but the word commonly refers to the hookah and hookah tobacco.

What else changes in Vietnam

  • Selling tobacco through online shops and e-commerce platforms is banned, except for deals between licensed businesses.
  • Tobacco vending machines are banned.
  • Sellers must refuse a sale if the buyer cannot prove they are 18 or older.
  • The grounds of all educational institutions, including universities, become completely smoke-free.
  • Displaying cigarette packs in shops will be banned from 1 January 2028, according to DTiNews.
  • Health warnings on packs will get bigger; the government will set the exact size.

Ordinary cigarettes remain legal: their production and sale are still allowed under licence. A "generational" ban for people born after 2010, which had been debated, was not included.

A day earlier, on 20 November, deputies also amended the Investment Law, removing business in e-cigarettes, heated tobacco, nicotine pouches and shisha from the list of permitted activities.

What it means for Serbia and Belgrade

Nothing changes for Serbia: Vietnamese law applies only in Vietnam. Hookah lounges in Belgrade operate under Serbian rules; we covered where you can smoke hookah in Serbia in a separate guide.

The practical takeaway is for travellers. If you are planning a trip to Vietnam after 1 July 2027, bear in mind that shisha will be banned there not only in venues but also for personal use. Do not pack a hookah, hookah tobacco or electronic devices. The reports on the new law do not yet state the size of the penalties.

The news fits a wider trend: more and more countries are tightening the rules on hookah and new nicotine products. We will keep following changes in the EU, the region and around the world.

FAQ

When does Vietnam's hookah ban start?

On 1 July 2027, when the new tobacco law takes effect. It bans the production, sale, import, storage and use of shisha.

Can tourists smoke hookah in Vietnam after 2027?

No. The law also bans the use of shisha, so the rule applies to everyone in the country.

Are ordinary cigarettes banned in Vietnam?

No. Cigarettes stay legal under licence, but sales rules get stricter: no online sales, vending machines or pack displays.

Will Vietnam's law affect hookah lounges in Belgrade?

No. It applies only in Vietnam, and hookah lounges in Serbia operate under Serbian rules.

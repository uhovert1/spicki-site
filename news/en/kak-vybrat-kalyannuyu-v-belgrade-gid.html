How to Choose a Hookah Lounge in Belgrade: 7 Things to Look For

23 November 2026

Belgrade has plenty of hookah and shisha bars, and picking one from a single social media photo is a gamble. Here are seven simple signs of a good place, plus what Serbian law says about it.

1. Who prepares the hookah

The most important person in a hookah lounge is whoever packs the bowl. A good hookah master asks what you like, picks the tobacco and strength for the whole group rather than just the most experienced smoker, and watches the coals all evening. If coals are only changed when a guest asks, the flavour fades fast. Ask at the door who is in charge of the hookahs and how often they check on tables.

2. The sign on the door and the law

Smoking in Serbian bars and restaurants is governed by the 2010 law on protection from tobacco smoke. In venues up to 80 m², the owner decides whether the room is smoking or non-smoking and must mark it on the entrance door. In venues larger than 80 m², at least half of the floor area must be non-smoking, located by the entrance. So the first thing to check is the sign: it tells you straight away whether smoking is allowed inside. We covered the law in detail in a separate guide.

3. Tobacco is sold with the service

According to 021.rs and Euronews Srbija, the 2023 amendments to Serbia's Tobacco Act ban offering hookah as a service in venues unless water-pipe tobacco is sold along with it. A legitimate shisha bar works with legal tobacco and won't ask you to bring your own.

4. Adults only

The same law explicitly bans selling tobacco and related products to minors. A place that asks young-looking guests for ID is showing it plays by the rules.

5. Kitchen and bar

A hookah session means a long evening, so food and drinks matter. Check the menu in advance: is there proper hot food, not just snacks, plus cocktails, wine and soft drinks?

6. Things to do with friends

Board games, consoles, private tables or a separate room turn a hookah bar into a place for a birthday or a night out with friends. Ask whether such a table can be booked ahead.

7. Booking, payment and location

Good places fill up on Fridays and Saturdays, so online booking saves time. Check whether cards are accepted, what the opening hours are and how to get there: the centre, Vračar and the area around the Church of Saint Sava are convenient for tourists and locals alike.

What this means for guests in Belgrade

Serbia has no separate law for hookah lounges: general rules for cafés and bars apply, and a full indoor smoking ban in hospitality venues has still not been adopted. The choice is yours, so look at the quality of service, how honest the venue is and the atmosphere.

FAQ

Can you smoke hookah in cafés in Belgrade?

Yes, if the venue is marked as smoking under the 2010 law (rooms up to 80 m²) or has a smoking area. Check the sign on the entrance door.

What is the minimum age for a hookah lounge in Serbia?

Selling tobacco and related products to minors is banned in Serbia, so hookah is for guests aged 18+ only.

Do I need to book a hookah lounge in Belgrade?

On weekdays you can usually walk in, but on Fridays and Saturdays it's best to book a table in advance, especially for a group.

Hookah birthday in Belgrade: how to plan a party at a hookah lounge

24 November 2026

A hookah lounge is a handy place for a birthday: a long evening, food, games and hookah for the whole group. Here are seven things to arrange in advance and the rules that apply in Serbia.

Why celebrate at a hookah lounge

Hookah is an unhurried format: one bowl shared by the group, conversation, food and games, with nobody rushing. That is why many people pick a hookah lounge in Belgrade for a birthday instead of a club or a restaurant. A few things are worth sorting out in advance so the evening goes smoothly.

1. Book early and give an exact headcount

On Fridays and Saturdays the good tables in Belgrade fill up quickly. Book a few days ahead and say straight away how many people are coming: it decides which table or room suits you and how many hookahs you will need.

2. Check everyone's age

In Serbia it is illegal to sell tobacco and related products to minors, and hookah lounges are 18+. If teenagers are part of the group, choose another venue or format in advance: a responsible lounge will ask younger guests for ID, and nobody wants an argument at the door on the big day.

3. Find out where smoking is allowed

Smoking in cafés and bars is governed by Serbia's 2010 law on protection from tobacco smoke. In venues up to 80 m² the owner marks the whole room as smoking or non-smoking with a sign on the door; in larger venues at least half the floor area must be non-smoking. If some of your guests don't smoke, ask where your table will be and how the ventilation works.

4. Agree on hookahs for the whole group

Tastes differ around one table: some people smoke rarely, others like it stronger. Tell the hookah master right away so they can pick the tobacco and strength that suit everyone, not just the most experienced guest. It also helps to decide how many bowls you want to start with and how often to refresh them.

5. Food, cake and drinks

A birthday is a long night, so a proper kitchen matters more than snacks. Check in advance whether you can bring your own cake and whether there are soft drinks for those who are driving.

6. Keeping guests entertained

Board games, consoles or a separate room turn a get-together into a party: while some smoke and chat, others play. If you want your group to be apart from the main room, ask about a private space.

7. Getting there and home

Pick a place everyone can reach easily: Vračar and the area around the Temple of Saint Sava are close to the city centre. Check the closing time in advance so the party doesn't end halfway through.

What this means for guests in Belgrade

Serbia has no special rules for parties at hookah lounges: the general rules for cafés and bars apply — the 80 m² rule, signs on the door and the ban on selling tobacco to minors. Everything else is a matter of arrangement with the venue, so the earlier you start, the calmer the evening.

FAQ

Can I celebrate a birthday at a hookah lounge with guests under 18?

No: Serbia bans the sale of tobacco and related products to minors, and hookah lounges are for guests 18+ only.

How far ahead should I book a hookah lounge in Belgrade for a birthday?

For Fridays and Saturdays, book a few days ahead and give the exact number of guests.

Can non-smokers sit in a hookah lounge?

It depends on the venue: under the 2010 law, rooms up to 80 m² are marked smoking or non-smoking, and larger venues have a non-smoking zone. Ask when booking.
